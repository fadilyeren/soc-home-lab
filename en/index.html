SOC Home Lab

I built this lab to prepare myself for real environments by simulating events that happen in them. Using Splunk, Sysmon and Atomic Red Team, I ran five attacks, investigated the traces they left with SPL, and wrote a detection for each one.

My lab had false positives too, just like a real environment. Most of my time went into filtering out logs other than the ones I wanted; dealing with that is also what I'm most proud of. On each page, next to the queries I wrote, I explain the mistakes I made and how I fixed them. I turned the brute-force detection into a scheduled alert and triggered it.

Scenarios 1-4 were run on the Windows VM with Atomic Red Team, scenario 5 from Kali over the network. Logs land in the wineventlog and sysmon indexes in Splunk.

Scenarios

  • 01 Discovery commands T1033 · T1087.001 · T1082 3 different discovery tools in 3 minutes. A sliding window with dc(Image).
  • 02 Encoded PowerShell T1059.001 · T1027 -EncodedCommand and its abbreviations. From 203 events to 1 in 7 days.
  • 03 Adding a member to Administrators T1098 · T1136.001 4732 and the language-independent group SID S-1-5-32-544.
  • 04 Persistence via Run keys T1547.001 Run, RunOnce and RunOnceEx with Sysmon 13. What to filter out and what to keep.
  • 05 Brute force over SMB T1110.001 netexec from Kali. A scheduled alert was set up and triggered.

All tests were run in my own isolated lab. No real malware was used.
